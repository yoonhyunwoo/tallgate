Features

Choose what gets blocked

Toggle blocking per feed. Following feeds, search, and direct messages keep working normally.

Instagram Reels

Redirects the home For you tab back to Feed and closes fullscreen Reels with the system back action.

Preserves DM chats

Instagram Explore

Redirects back to the home feed when the Explore tab is opened.

Disabled by default

YouTube Shorts

Triggers the system back action when the Shorts player opens. Standard videos play normally.

TikTok For You feed

Exits the For You feed upon entry.

Haptic and toast alerts

Configurable vibration and brief toast notifications whenever a feed is blocked.

Zero data collection

Requests no internet permission and cannot transmit screen content off-device. All settings stay local.
