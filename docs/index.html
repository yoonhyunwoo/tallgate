Granular control over what gets blocked

Toggle individual short-form features per app. Regular feeds, search, and active DM chats remain intact.

📷

Instagram Reels

Redirects the home For you tab back to Feed and closes fullscreen Reels viewers using the system back action.

DM chats excluded
🔍

Instagram Explore

Redirects back to the home feed when the Explore (Search) tab is opened. Disabled by default.

Optional
▶️

YouTube Shorts

Immediately navigates back when the Shorts player is launched. Standard videos play normally.

🎵

TikTok For You Feed

Exits the endless For You recommendation feed upon entry.

📳

Haptic & Toast Alerts

Provides subtle vibration and brief toast notifications on block events, individually configurable.

🔒

Zero Data Collection

Requires no internet permission and cannot transmit screen content off-device. All settings stay local.
