TallGate exits short-form video feeds the moment they open, helping you avoid unintentional video scrolling.

[Supported feeds]
• Instagram: redirects the home 'For you' tab back to 'Feed' and closes fullscreen Reels with the system back action. Active DM chats are preserved.
• Instagram Explore: redirects back to the home feed when the Explore tab is opened (disabled by default).
• YouTube Shorts: exits the Shorts player immediately using the system back action. Standard videos play normally.
• TikTok: exits the 'For You' feed upon entry.

[Features and privacy]
• Per-feed controls: turn blocking on or off independently for each supported feed.
• Block alerts: optional vibration and brief toast notifications whenever a feed is dismissed.
• No network access: TallGate omits the android.permission.INTERNET permission. No data leaves your device, and there are no accounts, advertisements, or analytics trackers.
• Local storage: your preferences stay strictly in private app storage on your device.

[Accessibility service disclosure]
TallGate uses Android's AccessibilityService API to detect and exit short-form video feeds in supported apps.

• Information accessed: while Instagram, YouTube, or TikTok is active, TallGate inspects UI node identifiers, view labels, and tab selection states to identify short-form screens. Events from all other apps are ignored.
• Actions taken: performs a system back action or switches to the standard feed tab when a short-form screen is detected, and triggers your configured alert.
• Data protection: TallGate does not capture, record, log, or transmit on-screen content or personal information. Evaluation occurs in memory only.

The service requires explicit consent before activation and can be disabled at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility > TallGate.
